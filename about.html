About

A mill built on consistency.

ARC Egypt is an Egyptian flour mill. We turn wheat into flour for bakeries, pastry makers, food manufacturers and distributors.

We keep it simple: buy good grain, mill it carefully, test the result, and deliver on the day we promised.

Grain selection
Wheat is inspected and tested before it enters the mill.
Milling
Controlled milling for the right extraction rate for each use.
Quality control
Regular checks so each batch matches the last.
Delivery
Packed in bags or bulk and delivered to your door.
